# Blueprint OS 2.0 — Product Specification

## Purpose
One private place for James to run his personal life, leadership priorities and high-level business focus without duplicating the operational business dashboard.

## Core structure
1. Home — today’s focus and life pulse
2. Daily — three-minute morning and two-minute evening workflow
3. Me — mood, confidence, reflection, gratitude and avoidance
4. Relationships — connection actions, promises and trends
5. Health — sleep, energy, stress and habits
6. Goals — personal, relationship, health, growth and work goals
7. CEO — opportunity, risk, decision, delegation and headline KPIs
8. Analytics — patterns across life and performance
9. Weekly — review and reset
10. Business Hub — integration layer for the separate business dashboard
11. Settings — mission and standards

## Design rules
- Personal-first, with CEO information available but not dominant.
- One page, one purpose.
- Minimal typing wherever possible.
- No duplicate operational business data.
- Every chart must support a decision or reflection.
- Existing Supabase data remains the source of truth.

## Roadmap
### 2.0 Foundation
New navigation, personal home, Me centre, CEO centre, analytics and business integration layer.

### 2.1 Faster daily use
Quick check-in controls, morning/evening modes, auto-save and mobile optimisation.

### 2.2 Life balance
Seven-domain balance view, personal finance pulse, fun/recovery planning and alerts.

### 2.3 Calendar and reminders
Outlook calendar connection, important dates and daily prompts.

### 2.4 Business dashboard integration
Secure import/API connection for headline KPIs and deep links.

### 2.5 Stevie insights
Rules-based insights first; optional AI analysis later with explicit privacy controls.
