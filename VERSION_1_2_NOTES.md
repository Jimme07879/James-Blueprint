# Version 1.2 Release Notes

This update focuses on consistency and measurable progress.

## Daily
- Today's cloud record loads automatically.
- A live completion percentage shows whether the worksheet is genuinely finished.
- Consecutive check-in streaks reward consistency.

## Weekly
- The Weekly page automatically summarises overall score, missions, exercise and smoke-free days.
- Saved reviews remain available below the current review.

## Habits
- Exercise and no-smoking streaks are calculated from consecutive saved daily records.
- Thirty-day totals remain visible for context.

## Goals
- Status totals and overall completion percentage.
- Deadline warnings for overdue items.
- One-click completion.

No SQL changes are needed.
